Movies

The Babysitter

Entry one

Genre: Horror/Comedy, Length: 1 hour 20 minutes, Streaming platform: Netflix, rating: TV-MA

Yesterday, my friends and I were looking for a horror movie to watch. We were scrolling through Netflix until we landed on the babysitter movie. We watched the trailer; we liked it and decided to watch the movie.

Characters: The babysitter: Bee 12 year old boy: Cole 12 year old boy's friend: Melanie

Summary

The movie starts off with a very shy boy called Cole. At school, he is a bit of an outcast, as he gets bullied by his peers. At home, we can see that he doesn't like taking risks - he has the chance to drive a super cool race car, but he is too afraid. Cole is also one of the only kids his age who still has a babysitter. Cole and his babysitter really get along; they play a lot of games together, and his babysitter even fights off his bullies for him.

As time passes, in some instances we can tell that Cole has a crush on his babysitter, Bee. Now, Cole heads to school the next day and talks with his friend, Melanie. Melanie tells him that once he goes to sleep, his babysitter probably spends time with other boys and her friends, because that's what all babysitters do. Along with that, Cole's parents were going out of town this weekend. So cole decided to find out for himself if Bee spends time with others inside his house. The day passes; Bee and Cole have a lot of fun together, then Bee puts Cole to bed, but Cole decides to stay awake. After waiting for a while, he decides to hide behind the stairs and look towards the living room. In the living room, his prediction was correct; he saw Bee with a bunch of her friends. They were playing a game. Cole kept watching, and now the twist happens. Are you ready? Bee takes out 2 knives and kills her friend. Bee was a murderer! Cole was shocked and decided to go to his room and call the police. He overheard Bee and her friends coming to take his blood next(for what reason you may ask? Well, we don't know yet), and he pretends to be asleep. They take his blood, and then Cole tries to make a run, but Bee is right behind him and catches him in the act. Now he is tied up in a chair in his living room. The cops arrive, but Bee and her Friends are Maniacs and kill the cops. Now Cole has to fight off Bee and her friends all by himself; he somehow manages to do that, and his parents come to save him.

Filling in the gaps | Explanations

While Cole was coming home from school, one day he saw Bee flirting with another guy. This happened to be the guy that she killed in the living room. The rest were her friends. We could kind of see this coming, because Bee was way out of that guy's league, and she was obviously flirting with him for a reason. How did Cole get away from Bee and all of her friends? We know from the start of the movie that Cole is a bookworm. He has insane skills and knows how to operate a variety of different things, and he uses that knowledge to trick Bee's friends.

He uses his intelligence to get away from Bee's friends and make weapons that he uses to protect himself. Of course, a LOT of luck is on Cole's side. You can say that the whole plot of the movie revolves around the theme of getting out of the comfort zone and risk-taking. Why? Because at the end of the movie, Cole finally races the car into his house(sounds kind of crazy, I know), and ends up crashing into Bee and killing her. Additionally, another thing that doesn't make sense was why didn't more cops come in after Bee and her friends killed two of the policemen? Basically, Bee asked Cole for the codes to signal "all clear"(code used to indicate the cops that the mission was successfull) to the other officers on the remote they have. Under pressure, Cole had no choice but to tell them the codes. This allowed Bee to signal to the cops that the site was all clear. This still doesn't make sense to me: how they weren't able to recognize the cops voice, or there must have been some voice input on the cops' remote. This point in the movie was where the things didn't seem tied up for me. If you are wondering why Bee sacrificed her friend and wanted the blood of Cole. The explanation is coming up next: Bee is part of this cult. She believes that whatever she wishes for will come true if she pours the blood of someone innocent on top of the bible with the blood of someone who's been sacrificed. So the person Bee was flirting with was the one whose blood had been sacrificed, and Cole had the blood of someone who was innocent. However, then again this is where the story's ends are kind of loose for me. Cole wanted Bee as a girlfriend, so he wasn't as innocent as we think. This implies that no matter what, the spell wouldn't have worked, and all of Bee's effort would end up wasted. Another loose end of the movie was when Bee caught Cole with her friend. However, She ended up not shooting Cole but just shooting her friend instead? Bee didn't even hurt Cole. Despite Cole knowing all about her secret murders, and killing all of her friends. This means that despite doing all this, Bee actually had a good heart on the inside, and had feelings for Cole. The final concern that I have is for Cole. Which is how would he explain this crazy story to his parents, who were on the trip! Hahaha.

Final Verdict

Now I will begin to list my ratings of the movie with explanations. In this blog, I am going to rate the movie based on some categories that I have selected. The criteria I have curated will include Story, Characters, Acting Performances, Pacing, Villain, Ending, Personal Enjoyment.

Story

The movie had a very unique concept - babysitter turned murderer, and at the start of the movie we couldn't even tell that Bee could be a murderer. It was masked pretty well. There were some plot holes, and some things that didn't make sense, as mentioned above, so that will take a bit of a negative impact on the rating of the story. Overall, I would rate the plot/story a 7/10.

Characters

I think this movie had a complete range of characters. The biggest character development was visible in the character of Cole. From being too scared to move the car one inch, to fully ramming it into the house to kill Bee - I think you can agree that sounds like insane character development. The babysitter and her friends were very diverse; one of her friends was a cheerleader, one was a jacked jock, one of her friends was an artist, and there was also a funny dude. The concept of the character that the babysitter had and how she became the murderer was really cool and completely unexpected, so I would say that the characters in this movie were excellent. Overall, I would rate the characters a 10/10.

Acting performances

Despite this being the actor of Cole: Judah's first movie and at a very young age. He was able to portray the character of a scared, nerd child and an unstoppable killing force very well. His acting was fantastic. The babysitter, the actress's name is Samara, had an amazing performance. This was also one of her very first movies. The best acting scene in the movie was when she called her friend to Cole's house and seduced him and immediately killed him after; the transition from romantic to killer was insane. Additionally, the babysitter's other friend, King Bach, did some awesome comedy and made us all laugh. So I think we all can agree that the acting performances were impeccable. Overall, I would rate the acting performances a 10/10.

Pacing

The movie's runtime was 1 hour and 20 minutes, so it was a reasonably short movie. An average movie's runtime is 1 hour 40 to 1 hour 55 minutes. The time went by pretty fast as it was a fast-paced action movie. However, the start was too slow, which is why I will deduct a few points from this category. Overall, I would rate the pacing of the movie an 8/10.

Villain

The choice of the villain being the babysitter was a very unique part of this movie. It was totally unexpected and a good twist. However, the villain didn't try their very best; they had an opportunity to kill Cole, but they let their emotions take over, and the plot was against the villains as well. I would say that the villains were reasonably weak in this movie and kept getting super unlucky. This made it feel too easy for the hero, and that's why I would deduct points from the villains. Overall, I would rate the villains of the movie a 6/10.

Ending

At the end, I was expecting at least some twist or some sort of major event to happen. But this movie broke my expectations. There was no twist, and at the end, the hero, Cole simply defeated the villain, Bee. The story also ended without fully tying up all the knots and answering all our question which was unsatisfying. I was also super surprised, how there could be another movie, when he literally killed the main villain: Bee. It wasn't like the babysitter escaped or anything. Overall, I would rate the ending of this movie a 4/10.

Personal enjoyment

This movie wasn't like a typical horror movie. There weren't many jumpscares, or scary scenes overall. I would say it was more gorey, and it was very hard to get scared. This is because whenever the scary scenes came, there was text that came on the screen which was very goofy and it made it impossible not to laugh. Also one time, when cole's bully came the babysitter's friend had almost got him, but let him go just to catch his bully. Which didn't make any sense at all. Due to such unexplained portions of the movie, gore, and comedic effects took away from the horror aspect of the movie. However, it was still enjoyable to watch and I felt entertained for a majority of the film after the starting 20 minutes. The starting 20 minutes were very boring because they were trying to setup the plot. It would have been much better if they just jumped right into the action in my opinion. Overall, I would rate the personal enjoyment of this movie a 5/10.

Rating

Adding all of these numbers up, the final rating ends up being (7+10+10+8+6+4+5)/70, which is a 7.1/10

Final Rating: 7.1/10
